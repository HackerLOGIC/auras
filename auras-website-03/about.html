AURAS International

Welcome to AURAS

Dear Friend, AURAS INTERNATIONAL LIMITED is a comprehensive international trade group headquartered in Hong Kong. We serve overseas markets with professional trade services, quality own-brand products, industrial production line projects and new energy solutions.

With a People Power culture at our core, we combine experience, systems and partnerships to deliver value to customers around the world.

Business Overview

What We Do

AURAS coordinates sourcing, quality control, logistics and delivery - one partner for complete international trade.

Vision & Business Overview

We are committed to building a professional bridge between international buyers and reliable manufacturers. Our vision is to provide honest, efficient and complete solutions for every customer request.

  • Trade services: purchasing, transport, QC, customs, translation, negotiation, order management
  • Logistics: sea forwarding, air forwarding and integrated shipment support
  • Products: stationery, food, home & office, auto parts, metal, radiators, valves, housing, dispensers
  • New energy: EV charging, solar, green hydrogen, crude oil, natural gas, medicine, car export
